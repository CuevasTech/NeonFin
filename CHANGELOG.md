# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-19

First public release.

- Cinematic detail pages: full-screen backdrop with a slow zoom, info column on the left, glass panels, and a gradient play button with a light sweep and a ripple.
- Glass top bar that fades out softly, with a slow aurora inside; it shrinks when you scroll.
- Library toolbar restyled (title with count, gradient "Play all" pill, glass controls), on a single line on mobile.
- Calm hover effects on posters: glow, small zoom, glass play button, buttons centred at the bottom.
- Minimal glass badges for unplayed counts and the watched mark.
- Themed tags with a gradient underline on hover, restyled text links, track selectors and action menus.
- Server name as a neon sign, or your own logo with `--nf-logo`.
- Colours and labels configurable with CSS variables.
- Mobile layout and the Android app's back button supported.
- Works with the system's "reduce motion" setting.
